I did a real quick translation of another chapter of Hatarakanai Futari! ([You can see all my previous translations here.](https://ewie.online/tags/mi-tu-li-pali-ala/)) It's been a few months since the last one. This one I was able to knock out after I got home from work. I think I'm improving at my pace.

In my last post, I mentioned wishing that toki pona had some sort of syllabery. There's a few systems that exist, but the only halfway good one is sitelen telo, which features a full logography and syllabery system. In my opinion, it still has a number of flaws, but it's the only one that somewhat approximates a more natural handwriting system for toki pona. I find writing sitelen pona by hand agonizing, but I was able to quickly do a version of it in sitelen telo in about an hour. Here's the sitelen telo version for you to read!

![Chapter 1957 of the the manga "Hatarakanai Futari". It's been translated into toki pona and written in sitelen telo.](https://cdn.ewie.online/Exactly%20Half%20sitelen%20telo.png)

I don't really have any real opinions on this chapter. It came out this week and made me chuckle when reading it and I thought it wouldn't be too hard to translate. The actual fun part of this is the lettering, the translation is the hard part I need to do to get to the fun part.
