This chapter is the second chapter I've translated! ([Here is the first.](https://ewie.online/posts/20250514-oki-i-did-it-here-s/)) This one has a whole different workflow. Previously, I would work on it in Affinity Photo 2, using sitelen pona toki pona fonts. The text in those would read from left to right, even though you'd read the panels in normal manga order. This would be a similar reading flow to reading manga translated in english, which isn't bad or anything. However, I found that reading sitelen pona in left to right on manga was harder and more confusing than reading english left to right in manga. Since I have pretty decent handwriting and have done some manga typesetting and lettering in the past, I thought it wouldn't be that hard to write all of it by hand.

So I did it! It kinda sucked to do it all by hand, but I've worked out a decent flow and it's still very pleasant and meditative. I'd estimate that handwriting 8 pages like this took about a week. Then I imported it into Affinity Photo to clean up layers and center the text in the speech bubbles, which took about a day. I asked someone in a toki pona discord to proofread it, and the proofreading and revising process took a few days, but then it was all done! It was a gargantuan amount of labor, but I think it was all worth it.

I have two main goals when doing this: 

The first was to create a "most authentic" version of a manga translation for this manga. The scanlation group for Hatarakanai Futari, unko mans, does an exceptional job translating this manga into English. It's one of those manga scanlation jobs where you could just sell their work as a tankoban and it wouldn't look out of place. It's official quality. I'd feel like I was letting down the prestige of the group if I was releasing a lower quality translation than they were. Moreover, this level of quality is what I'd imagine an official sitelen pona translation would use. I don't think they'd handwrite it all, but I think there'd be a font that a hypothetical official translation would use that would look similar to what I'm using. Sitelen pona fonts are very limited and all assume that you're writing from left to right, so I have to do it all myself. I also think that working in the same direction as the Japanese raws makes it really easy to fit all the dialogue into the bubbles. I never really feel like I'm struggling to fit everything in, which was a frequent problem I had when working in english.

The second goal was to just get more attention on Hatarakanani Futari! This is my favorite manga, bar none. When I was reading it for the first time, I cried around every 15 minutes. It's a page a day manga, so chapters like this would be slowly dished out to you over the course of a week (I think). Many chapters are just a page or two, but these longer ones are super impactful, and I think the overall length of the manga really serves to get you to understand these characters in a way few other manga can. The length is an asset!

I have many more thoughts about this, but I'll save them for future blog posts. The long and short of this is that I would be happy if this project got more people to read this manga.
